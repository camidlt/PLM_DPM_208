# Surface brief: master site (landing + role apps)

Scope: `templates/landing.html` is the Persuade front door for the jury; the role screens (garagiste, propriétaire, centre de tri, public passport) are Operate surfaces inside the same world. Code-led build (no image generation).

Audience and job: jury on a projector must grasp in seconds that a real ESP32 feeds a battery passport; staff roles complete the diagnosis, status, transfer, reception and decision flow on tablet, desktop and phone.

Constraints: French copy, no emoji, real drawn icons (Lucide), no em or en dashes in copy, no AI-default fonts, Capgemini and ESILV logos, Capgemini blues #0070AD and #12ABDB.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the battery pack itself, one machined anodized casing whose etched identity plate, five glass cell bays and single indicator light are the passport. It refuses the SaaS hero over a row of role cards.

OWN-WORLD: Bead-blasted anodized blue-black casing from one eleven-step ramp (#07111C to #E6EDF3), Capgemini blue #0070AD as anodized panels, vibrant #12ABDB reserved for live or actionable only, laser-etched light titanium plates (#C9D2DA) with dark engraved type, smoked-glass cell bays, a hot indicator (#FF6A3D) for critical. Michroma for etched plate lettering, Archivo (width axis) for everything read. Chamfered corners, 1px machined rims, fastener dots.

STORY: The visitor sees DLR-1985 breathing live from the ESP32, sees module 3 heat up, understands the chain garage to owner to sorting centre, and opens the passport or signs in to a role.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Casing top edge carries the ESILV and Capgemini lockup and links. Left five columns: etched plate with the title, the serial line, one sentence, and a working passport lookup field with its open button as primary action. Right seven columns: five cell bays at full height with live resistance, shared ruler, device status strip under them.

FORM: Machined Module Housing, candidate 6 of the ordered list, seed key dac44b05. Signature interaction: weighted ballistic cell readouts that heat from blue to warning to critical. Raises: shared ruler and ballistics (VU bridge), single reserved action colour (zine), eleven-step ramp (darkroom), provenance on every value (quilt), one health progression (glaze).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
